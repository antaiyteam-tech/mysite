Rules & Privacy

Platform services

We verify mentors and match members. After a match, how you work together, what you charge, and how you split profits is up to the two of you.

The matching fee

Basic 1.3 SOL, Intermediate 10.3 SOL, Advanced 100.3 SOL. The payment address changes daily. The matching fee is non-refundable.

How matching works

Matches are made in queue order, by level and by when we receive your fee. If there are fewer members than mentors, mentors wait; if there are fewer mentors, members wait.

Personal information

When we make a match, we share your name and email with the other party. We never sell your information. By checking the box and submitting, you agree to this page.
