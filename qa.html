Quick Q&A

Why join, how to join, and how to register for a match. Start here.

Why do members join antaiy AI Co-creation?

Social media accounts and shops are sleeping assets for most people, and there is a chance to wake them up so they can start to earn. But running them well is not easy: it takes many skills, and sometimes startup funds and ad spend. Platform rules and the fortunes of each niche keep changing, so most people cannot afford the cost of trial and error (time, money, energy), and no one can master every skill by studying alone. Co-creation lets a master mentor who is already getting results lead you in the niche he or she has proven, so you can claim your place in the market quickly and share in the upside together. Learn by doing and do by learning, and you can carry your own skills or assets over to run another account. If it works, then in the next cycle and the next niche, you could become the master mentor. This describes a goal, not an income promise, and results vary from person to person.

How do members join antaiy AI Co-creation?

Click Register, take stock of your accounts on platforms that are not earning yet, and briefly describe your past successes, special skills and resources (leave out private information). Our AI bot will register you and reply. Once a master mentor is willing to match with you (your different accounts and shops may match different mentors), we will email you the details. You can report each month how your accounts are growing and earning. If the numbers fall short of the targets you both agreed in advance and it is not your doing, we will do our best to help you swap your mentor (or platform). Your accounts always stay yours: never share your passwords with anyone, and use only the collaborator or sub-account permissions the platform allows.

Why do master mentors join antaiy AI Co-creation?

In any niche or business, the best-return window is usually very short, and you have probably spent real money building your own account matrix, with accounts often banned. The profit share is agreed between the two sides, for example 50% or more, so why not? With AI and support agents you can match with 100 or more members at the same time and start working together when a niche's window opens, which can go far beyond what you could do alone. If they want to learn, you can also charge your own training fees. Results vary and are not guaranteed.

How do master mentors join antaiy AI Co-creation?

Click Register and tell us about your successful platforms and shops, along with your supporting documents (you must have run it as your main business, earning more than US$300 a month over the last 3 months, and we cross-verify). Our AI bot will register you and reply. Once a member agrees to a match, you can set up the accounts and shops, using the collaboration methods the platform allows, and send content to members on a regular schedule, in set quantities.

Why do chat support agents join antaiy AI Co-creation?

You help members who struggle with unprofessional replies, settle arguments and rude exchanges, and save members' time. You don't have to sit at a desk all day: you can reply and comment in your spare moments, replying within the identity and permissions the platform allows. Income comes mainly from members' tips (red packets) or profit sharing. If your comments earn more than 1,000 likes, email us and we will upgrade your support-agent level.

How do members, master mentors and support agents register for a match?

Anyone can register for free and get a match number. If you would like to support us, a US$10 red packet or 0.1 SOL (Solana) is welcome. It is entirely voluntary, does not affect your match, and is not a token sale or an investment. If you would also like to set aside a little of your monthly co-creation profit share to support us, we would be very grateful. Such support helps us bring more master mentors into the community to share and speak, so that AI can reach every industry, community and household and free good people from tedious, repetitive and dangerous work.
